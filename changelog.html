Release notes

Version 1.0.0

First release.

  • Three presets: MOHINI (fashion), Dark (tech) and Soft (beauty).
  • Ten header styles and ten footer styles, mega menus with any number of promos (text below or on the image), menu labels, an announcement bar with countdowns, and a sticky or transparent header.
  • 45 sections you can add, including Hero slider with WebGL transitions, Zoom reveal, Runway, Showcase, Shop the look, Lookbook, Before / after, Timeline, Stats and Comparison.
  • Product page with 20 blocks, including pickup availability, complementary products, countdown, delivery estimate, tabs and highlights; image zoom, video and 3D models; quick view and quick add.
  • Filters and sorting from Search & Discovery, predictive search, and a quick order list for bulk and B2B orders.
  • Cart drawer, page or popup, with a free shipping bar, gift wrap and gift messages.
  • GSAP animations that load on first interaction, per-section styles, and support for reduced motion.
  • Storefront text in English, French, German, Italian and Spanish, and right-to-left languages.
