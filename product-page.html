Product page

The product page has a media gallery and a column of blocks. Add, remove and reorder blocks in the theme editor; each block has its own settings.

Media

Product media can be images, videos and 3D models. In the section's Media settings, choose the desktop layout: a grid, thumbnails on the left, a slider with thumbnails or stacked images. Phones always get a swipeable slider. Theme settings › Product › Enable image zoom opens images full screen. Variant images switch automatically when a customer picks a variant.

Buy buttons

Renders the quantity selector, add to cart button and dynamic checkout ("Buy it now") buttons. For the product section; the sticky add to cart bar submits this block's form. When the selected variant is sold out, a "Notify me" form can collect the customer's email. It uses Shopify's contact form, so the request (product, variant and link) arrives in the store's inbox.

Gift cards

Gift card products show a form so customers can send the card to someone else by email, with a message and a delivery date. The gift card page customers receive uses the theme's colors and fonts.

Quick view and quick add

Turn these on in Theme settings › Product. Quick add adds a product to the cart from its card (or opens a variant picker); quick view opens the product in a drawer with its gallery, variant picker and buy buttons.
