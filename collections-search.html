Collections and search

Filters

Filters come from Shopify's Search & Discovery app: install it, then choose which filters to show (availability, price, product type, vendor, variant options, metafields). In the collection and search sections, turn on Enable filtering and choose the filter layout: vertical (a sidebar that can start open, or a drawer opened from the Filter button) or horizontal (a bar of dropdowns). On phones, filters always open in a drawer, and results update without reloading the page.

Collection page

The collection template has a collection banner (title, description and image) and the product grid. Choose columns on desktop and phones, products per page, pagination (numbers, a "Load more" button or infinite scroll) and an optional menu of collections above the grid.

Collections list page

The Collections list section shows the collections you pick, or all of them in the order you choose. Layouts: editorial (first card large), grid, carousel, hover list or circles; cards can show the text on the image, below it or in a framed card.

Search

Predictive search shows products, collections, pages and articles as customers type (Theme settings › Search). The search results page has tabs for result types, filters, sorting, trending and recent searches, and suggestions when nothing matches.

Product grid

The collection page grid: filters, sorting, product cards and pagination, with an optional menu of collections above it.

Search

Search results page: a large search field with live results, tabs for result types, filters and sorting, a grid of products, articles and pages, and pagination. Before a search, and when nothing is found, it shows recent and popular searches, popular collections and suggested products. Filtering, sorting, "Load more" and infinite scroll use the same <collection-filters> element as the collection page (snippets/collection-filters.liquid), and the same toolbar, drawer and grid styles (sections/collection.liquid). Results refresh in place through the Section Rendering API; without JavaScript every form and link still works as a normal page load.
