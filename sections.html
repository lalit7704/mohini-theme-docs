Sections

Sections are the building blocks of each page. In the theme editor, choose Add section on any page to add one, drag to reorder, and use the eye icon to hide one without deleting it.

Sections marked Template section belong to a page type (like the product page) and can't be added elsewhere.

Zoom revealUp to 3 blocks

Zoom reveal: a pinned, scroll-driven opening for a campaign or collection. A large word is cut out of the section's color, so the image shows through its letters. As the customer scrolls, the camera dives into the thickest stroke of a letter until the image fills the screen; then the heading rises word by word, followed by the text, the button and up to three glass cards (highlights or products). On desktop the image drifts with the cursor, like a view through a window. It needs the theme's animations (GSAP). Without them, or when the customer prefers reduced motion, it's a full-screen image with the content on top.

RunwayUp to 8 blocks

Runway: models in a row on the left, their products in a column on the right. With animations on, the section pins while the customer scrolls: the models walk left to right and the products rise, one look at a time. The product at the top of the column is always the one worn by the model beside it, who is in focus; the models still to come wait smaller and blurred, and each model walks out behind the column when its turn is over. Without animations (or with reduced motion) the models swipe sideways and the product column scrolls on its own.

Contact form

Contact page: an intro with a reply-time badge, contact method cards (call, email, WhatsApp, visit, chat or any link), the contact form, and store details with opening hours, social links and a Google map. The form is Shopify's contact form, so messages arrive at the store's sender email. Extra "Form field" blocks are sent along with their label. The section script checks fields as customers go, counts characters, keeps an unsent message in the customer's browser, and fills in the topic from a link like /pages/contact?topic=Returns. Without JavaScript the form still sends, using the browser's own checks.

Promo popup

Promo popup: a dialog announcing a sale or offer. It sits in the middle of the screen or slides in at a bottom corner, and on phones it can rise from the bottom as a sheet. It can show an image (beside, above or behind the text), a round offer badge, perks, a countdown to the offer's end, a discount code on a coupon to copy (also applied at checkout), and an email signup or a button. The code can stay locked until the visitor signs up. It opens after a delay, when the visitor scrolls, or when they move to leave (exit intent), and stays away for a number of days once closed, while a small teaser in the corner can open it again. After a signup it doesn't show again. In the theme editor it opens while the section is selected.

Search

Search results page: a large search field with live results, tabs for result types, filters and sorting, a grid of products, articles and pages, and pagination. Before a search, and when nothing is found, it shows recent and popular searches, popular collections and suggested products. Filtering, sorting, "Load more" and infinite scroll use the same <collection-filters> element as the collection page (snippets/collection-filters.liquid), and the same toolbar, drawer and grid styles (sections/collection.liquid). Results refresh in place through the Section Rendering API; without JavaScript every form and link still works as a normal page load.
