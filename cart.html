Cart

Choose how the cart opens in Theme settings › Cart › Cart type.

Cart typeWhat happens on "Add to cart"
DrawerThe cart slides in from the side, with line items, the free shipping bar and checkout.
PageCustomers go to the cart page.
Popup notificationA small confirmation appears with links to the cart and checkout.

Free shipping bar

Turn on Show free shipping bar and set Free shipping minimum in your store's currency. The bar shows how much more customers need to spend, then tells them when they've unlocked free shipping. Set up the matching free shipping rate in Shopify admin › Settings › Shipping.

Gift wrap

Create a product for gift wrapping (for example "Gift wrap", with its price), then choose it in Gift wrap product. Customers can add it from the cart with one click, and leave a gift message if Let customers add a gift message is on.

Order notes and checkout buttons

Enable order notes adds a note field to the cart. Accelerated checkout buttons (Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay…) appear on the cart page when they're turned on in Shopify admin › Settings › Payments.
