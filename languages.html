Languages

The storefront text built into MOHINI (buttons, labels, messages) comes in English, French, German, Italian and Spanish.

  • Add languages in Shopify admin › Settings › Languages. Your own content (products, pages, section text) is translated with the Translate & Adapt app.
  • To change any built-in text, go to Online Store › Themes › … › Edit default theme content.
  • Right-to-left languages (Arabic, Hebrew, Persian, Urdu and others) are supported: the layout, menus, sliders and arrows mirror automatically. Translate the theme's text with Translate & Adapt.
  • Customers switch language and currency with the pickers in the header and footer (turn them on in the header's "Country and language" settings and the footer's bottom bar).
