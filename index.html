MOHINI theme documentation

MOHINI is a Shopify theme for fashion, tech and beauty brands, with ten header and footer styles, scroll-driven sections and fast product pages. This guide covers setup, every setting and section, and how to get help.

Before you edit the theme's code, duplicate the theme. In Shopify admin, go to Online Store › Themes, open the … menu next to MOHINI and choose Duplicate. Work on the copy. Theme updates can't carry over changes made to the code, and support doesn't cover edited code.

Install the theme

  1. In the Shopify Theme Store, open MOHINI and choose Try theme (or buy it). It's added to your theme library without replacing your current theme.
  2. Pick the preset closest to your brand. You can change every color, font and section later.
  3. Open Customize to edit it in the theme editor. Your live store doesn't change until you publish.
  4. When you're happy, choose Publish.

Setup checklist

  1. Logo and favicon. Header › Logo, and Theme settings › Favicon.
  2. Colors and fonts. Theme settings › Colors (color schemes) and Typography. See Theme settings.
  3. Menus. Build your main and footer menus in Online Store › Navigation, then pick them in the header and footer. For mega menus, see Mega menus.
  4. Home page. Add, remove and reorder sections. Replace every placeholder image and text with your own.
  5. Product page. Arrange the product blocks (price, variant picker, buy buttons, tabs…). See Product page.
  6. Collections, search and cart. Turn on filters in Search & Discovery, and choose a cart type. See Collections and search and Cart.
  7. Check on a phone. Use the device toggle at the top of the theme editor.

Images

  • Use large, sharp images: 2400 px wide for full-width banners, 1600 px for half-width images, 1200 px for product photos. Shopify serves the right size to each device.
  • Set a focal point on an image (in the image picker, choose Edit) so the important part stays in view when the image is cropped, especially on phones.
  • Many banners have a separate Mobile image setting for a portrait version.
  • Add alt text to images in Shopify admin so screen readers can describe them.

Getting help

Most answers are in this guide and the FAQ. If you're stuck, contact support: we reply within 2 business days.
