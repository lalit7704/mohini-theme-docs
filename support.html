Support

We're here to help with setting up and using MOHINI. We reply to every request within 2 business days (Monday to Friday).

Contact us

You can also email lalit979932@gmail.com.

Support policy

What's included

  • Help with installing MOHINI and using its settings, sections and blocks.
  • Fixing bugs in the theme, at no cost.
  • Guidance on images, presets and Shopify features the theme uses.

What's not included

  • Changes to the theme's code, or problems caused by code that's been edited.
  • Problems with third-party apps; please contact the app's developer.
  • Design work, store setup services or custom features.

To help us help you

  • Include your store URL and, if possible, a link to a preview of the theme.
  • If we need to look at your store, we'll ask for collaborator access; we never ask for your password.

Duplicate the theme before editing its code, and keep a copy you haven't edited. Support can't cover edited code.
