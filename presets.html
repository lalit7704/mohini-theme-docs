Presets

A preset is a starting point: a set of colors, fonts, header and footer styles and home page sections. All presets share the same features.

Switching presets

Presets are chosen when you add the theme. To try another one, add MOHINI again from the Theme Store with the other preset; your current copy stays in the theme library.

You can get any preset's look by hand too: the header and footer each have ten styles (Header › Style and Footer › Style), and colors and fonts are in Theme settings.
